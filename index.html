I specialize in building content systems that move the needle. Whether it's driving clicks, scaling engagement, or refining conversion funnels, I treat every asset as a measurable part of a growth machine.

My journey began with **10 years in Broadcast Subtitle Design**. In the world of high-stakes television, every word and frame is a battle for attention. I learned to design the flow of information with surgical precision—understanding exactly how to capture the viewer's eye and hold it.

I transitioned that "attention-first" mindset into **Customer Service and Sales**, where I learned the nuances of persuasion. Here, I discovered that content isn't successful unless it results in a tangible action. I moved from capturing attention to driving decision-making.

Today, I bridge these worlds in **K-beauty marketing in Canada**. By viewing marketing as a connected system—from the first Instagram impression to the final CRM retention flow—I translate brand vision into local commercial success.
