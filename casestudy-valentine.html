Strategic Evolution

edit_note

What I Changed

  • Decoupled Social & Direct Moved away from generic "Link in Bio" to specific Landing Page tags for every reel format.
  • Abandoned Cart Logic Introduced a 3-step recovery email specifically for Valentine bundles, offering a free gift wrap.
  • Mobile-First UI Redesigned the LPV to feature a 1-click checkout, reducing friction for Instagram users.
tips_and_updates

Why It Worked

  • Reduced Cognitive Load The 75.3% conversion rate was a result of removing all non-Valentine distractions from the landing page.
  • Psychological Urgency The "Phase 03" Close sequence accounted for 40% of total email revenue in the final 48 hours.
  • Creative Consistency Using the same editorial visual language from Reels to Inbox increased brand trust and retention.
